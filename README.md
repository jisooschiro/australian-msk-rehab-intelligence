# Australian MSK & Rehabilitation Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end healthcare data and AI research prototype focused on musculoskeletal rehabilitation and Australian allied-health workflows.

## Project Overview

Having worked clinically in musculoskeletal care, I wanted to explore how data and AI could support allied-health rehabilitation workflows.

This six-month portfolio project will progressively integrate:

* Clinic operations analytics
* Return-to-work outcome prediction
* Explainable machine learning
* Rehabilitation movement analysis
* Clinical evidence retrieval
* Healthcare interoperability and deployment

The project begins with **v0.1 — MSK Clinic Operations Analytics**.

## v0.1 — MSK Clinic Operations Analytics

The first version uses privacy-safe synthetic Australian MSK clinic data to analyse:

* Patient demographics
* MSK condition distribution
* Occupation and physical work demand
* Appointment attendance and no-shows
* Treatment sessions and duration
* Pain and function outcomes
* Return-to-work status
* Referral and provider patterns

## Problem Statement

Australian musculoskeletal and allied-health clinics generate operational and clinical outcome data across patients, referrals, appointments, treatment episodes, and return-to-work progress.

However, these data can be fragmented and difficult to translate into actionable insights.

This research prototype will use synthetic clinic data to examine patient and condition mix, service utilisation, attendance, treatment duration, pain and function improvement, and return-to-work outcomes.

The Month 1 result will be a reproducible analytics workflow using Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI.
